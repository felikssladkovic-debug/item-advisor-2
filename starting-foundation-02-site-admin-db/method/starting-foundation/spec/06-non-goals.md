# Non-goals

Do not implement:

- login;
- authentication;
- authorization;
- user management;
- roles and permissions;
- CRUD;
- admin dashboard;
- item catalog;
- item cards;
- search;
- complex UI;
- business entities;
- production business data model;
- business migrations;
- external APIs.
